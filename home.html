About Lithicon

Meeting Future Energy Demand With Excellence

In response to rising electricity demand driven by industrial growth and population increase, Lithicon aims to play a critical role in assisting to electrify emerging regions.

As an energy service provider and system integrator, we offer scalable, reliable energy systems tailored for local conditions. Our integrated solar PV and energy storage solutions allow commercial and industrial customers to replace costly diesel generators, mitigating carbon emissions while drastically reducing energy costs.

Products & Services

Tailored Power & Storage Solutions

Uninterrupted Backup Power

High-performance battery energy storage systems (BESS) paired with solar PV to maintain critical commercial and industrial operations during grid outages.

Off-Grid Microgrids

Integrated solar and energy storage systems supplying reliable electricity to remote areas without access to the central power grid.

Grid Ancillary Services

Advanced services including frequency regulation, voltage control, fault-current support, and reserves management to stabilize utility grids.
