Lithicon R&D Labs

Management Team

Our team consists of experts in engineering, economics, and finance. We bring together diverse perspectives and skills to tackle complex problems and develop creative solutions.

Co-Founder and Managing Partner

Andrew Ojede, Ph.D.

Professor Ojede is a Co-Founder and Managing Partner at Lithicon R&D Labs. He oversees the general operations of the company by setting short-, medium-, and long-term goals, and ensuring that the company's deliverables are accomplished.

With over 14 years of experience as a university professor and international scholar studying sources of growth financing for developing countries, Dr. Ojede brings significant knowledge of the economies of developing regions.

He earned his doctoral degree in economics from Kansas State University and consults with international organizations, including the World Bank and the United Nations.

Co-Founder and Adviser

Gilbert Malinga

Gilbert Malinga is a passionate entrepreneur with over 15 years of experience managing projects in industry and academia.

As a special adviser to senior leaders in business and government, he has provided advisory support on projects in energy, manufacturing, and infrastructure development in the United States and Africa.

Gilbert serves as a Technical Adviser to Lithicon R&D Labs, helping shape business strategy, strategic partnerships, and the firm's technology roadmap.

Technical Adviser

Mbaki Onyango, Ph.D.

Professor Mbaki Onyango serves as a Technical Adviser to Lithicon R&D Labs, supporting capacity-building programs and helping young people gain critical work skills.

Professor Onyango is the U.C. Foundation Professor in the Department of Civil and Chemical Engineering at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga.

She is an engineering educator, researcher, and consultant with expertise in transportation, construction materials, and STEM education.
